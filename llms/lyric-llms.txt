# Lyric

> Lyric is an AI-powered supply chain software company (founded 2022) whose Lyric Studio platform lets enterprises model, plan, and optimize their supply chains across four layers — Data, Algorithms, Workflows, and Models & Apps. Capabilities span network modeling, transportation optimization, inventory management, forecasting, routing, and scheduling. Lyric is a portfolio company of Insight Partners. No public developer API surface is published; the product documentation at docs.lyric.tech is gated behind a Lyric Studio login.
